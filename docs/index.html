The Ultimate Offline BMW Diagnostic Suite

Scan every module of your E-series BMW, read every fault and dash warning in plain English, test parts before you buy them, and code comfort options with an automatic backup. One cheap K+DCAN cable. No account, no subscription, no cloud.

Built for owners, not dealerships

Factory tools are made for workshops with a licence, a dedicated laptop and an afternoon to install them. Klemme15 is made for you and your car.

Installs in seconds

One small portable app: unzip and run, or one winget command. No multi-gigabyte install, no licence keys, no virtual machine. Runs on the laptop you already have.

FRM3 hardware safety gates

Klemme15 recognises the footwell module that corrupts itself on a voltage dip from its part number, and writes to it only on a charger. Every coding change is backed up, read back and put back automatically if anything is off.

Plain English fault translations

Every fault gets a meaning, sorted into fix now, worth checking and background. Hundreds of BMW codes and dash messages researched in our own words with sources, all standard OBD-II codes, and BMW's own text from your files when you have them.

See it work

Recorded on the built-in demo car, so you can try every one of these without a cable.

Digital Twin

Share your whole car in one file

Save a snapshot and attach it to your forum post. Whoever opens it explores your car as if they were plugged in: every module, every fault, the dash warning history, the last minute of live data. Read-only, and the VIN is removed everywhere.

  • Your note on top: what's wrong, what you tried
  • Live data replays on the Analyse page
Diagnostic Chains

A mechanic's reasoning, step by step

Lean codes? The Vacuum leak hunter compares your fuel trims at idle and 2,500 rpm, checks the airflow against your engine's size, asks you one question and points to the likely cause, with the numbers and graphs to back it up.

  • Written by the community, read-only by design
  • Offered right under the fault it explains
Coding with Auto-Restore

One click, and a way back

Back up a module's coding before anything touches it, switch comfort options like LED bulb-check off, and let Klemme15 read every block back. If anything doesn't match, the original goes back in automatically.

  • Comfort modules only: never the key, engine, brakes or airbags
  • Only settings Klemme15 knows exactly for your module

Lines we don't cross

Some things a free tool should never do to your car. Klemme15 doesn't do them, on purpose.
